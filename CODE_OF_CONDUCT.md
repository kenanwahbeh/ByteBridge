# Code of Conduct

ByteBridge is built in the open, in English and Arabic, by people with
very different backgrounds. This page says how we treat each other
while doing it. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation, and whichever
language they write in.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with someone's first contribution.
- Disagree about the code, not about the person. Say what you think is
  wrong and why.
- Accept review gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Own your mistakes and fix them.
- Write in the language you are comfortable in. Questions in Arabic are
  as welcome as questions in English.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses: issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@kenanwahbeh](https://github.com/kenanwahbeh),
by a message on GitHub. Reports are read by the maintainer only, and
the reporter's identity is kept private. Do not use a public issue for a
conduct report.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this page, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban a person from the project. The first response to most
problems will be a private word asking for the behaviour to stop.
Anything that is also a security problem goes through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
